# VCFEditor Android Companion - Real-World Feature Roadmap

Based on professional user needs, here are the proposed "Real-World" functional features to be implemented next.

## 1. Contact Intelligence & Cleanup
Utility tools to manage large contact lists effectively.
- **Duplicate Detection**: Identify contacts with similar names, identical phone numbers, or emails.
- **Batch Merge**: Merge duplicate contacts into a single entry with combined data.
- **Batch Delete**: Select multiple contacts to remove them at once.
- **Cleanup Suggestions**: Identify contacts without phone numbers, emails, or names.

## 2. Advanced Backup & Recovery
Reliable data protection and migration.
- **Scheduled Backups**: Automatically create local or cloud backups weekly/monthly.
- **Cloud Integration**: Export/Import backups directly from **Google Drive** or **Dropbox**.
- **Versioned Backups**: Keep multiple snapshots of contacts to roll back specific changes.

## 3. Contact Organization Tools
Better ways to categorize and find data.
- **Group Manager**: Create, rename, and delete contact groups (labels) directly from the app.
- **Advanced Filtering**: Filter contacts by Account Type (e.g., WhatsApp, Google, SIM), or by missing fields.
- **Company View**: View contacts grouped by the "Organization" field.

## 4. Enhanced File & Gallery Utility
Functional improvements for professional workflows.
- **Bulk Share**: Select multiple files or gallery items and share them as a single ZIP archive.
- **Built-in Document Viewer**: Preview PDF and text files directly inside the app without needing external apps.
- **Gallery "Privacy Folder"**: Move selected media items to an app-private storage area.

---

### Priority Phase: Contact Cleanup (The most requested "Real World" feature)
1.  **Duplicate Finder**: Scan the database for duplicates.
